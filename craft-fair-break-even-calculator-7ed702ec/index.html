How to read the numbers

Worked example: $60 in fixed costs, a $10 selling price and $6 variable cost leave $4 contribution per item. $60 ÷ $4 = 15 items to cover cash costs.

Add 5 hours valued at $12 per hour: time is $60, so ($60 + $60) ÷ $4 = 30 items including time. The cash target stays at 15.

  • Contribution = selling price − variable cost per item.
  • Cash target = fixed costs ÷ contribution, rounded up to a whole item.
  • Time-inclusive target = (fixed costs + total hours × hourly time value) ÷ contribution, rounded up. Fractional cents in time value are preserved.
  • Assumes one consistent price and variable cost per item, and that every counted item sells. For multiple products, calculate separately with an appropriate allocation of fixed costs.
  • Include applicable fees and taxes in your inputs. Costs of unsold inventory, discounts and other expenses are not added automatically. This is a planning estimate, not a sales forecast or guaranteed profit.
  • With zero contribution, sales cannot recover positive costs. With negative contribution, each sale increases the loss. If costs are zero, zero sales already cover them; that does not make loss-making sales profitable.
  • Reaching the rounded target covers the entered costs; it may leave a small surplus. Hours are treated as a fixed total for this event.
