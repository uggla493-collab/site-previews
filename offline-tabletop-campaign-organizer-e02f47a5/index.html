Your table, on one page

  1. Choose a campaign or create your own. The Tideglass Lantern is an original, editable example.
  2. Add sessions, NPCs, locations, and hooks. Save each edit. Resolve a hook when its story is complete.
  3. Search looks across every campaign. Open a result to edit the matching entry.
  4. After each session, choose Export backup. Keep the downloaded JSON in a folder you back up, ideally with a second copy elsewhere.
  5. To recover, choose Restore JSON, review the campaign counts, and confirm replacement. Invalid files never replace your archive.
  6. Print session previews a saved session with its summary, next steps, and the campaign's currently unresolved hooks. Private preparation notes are optional.

Browser storage is not a backup. Private windows, storage cleanup, a different browser, or a different file path may remove or hide your data. Export before moving or updating the app. Saves are local and unencrypted. Use one tab at a time.
